What Is Dental Bone Grafting?

A dental bone graft adds bone material to areas of the jaw that have lost volume because of tooth loss, gum disease, or injury. The graft works as a scaffold that supports bone regeneration, creating a stable base for future dental implants. A dental bone graft can use your own bone, donor bone, or synthetic material, and your dentist will recommend the option that fits your situation. Common procedures include socket preservation, ridge augmentation, sinus lift, and jawbone restoration.

Key Benefits of Dental Bone Graft Treatment

  • A stronger foundation for implants:

    • Bone grafting for implants helps restore the bone volume and density an implant needs. Careful implant preparation gives the implant a better chance of staying secure over time.

  • Protection after tooth loss:

    • A tooth loss bone graft or socket preservation procedure helps maintain the shape of your jaw after an extraction, so you keep more options for replacing the tooth later.

  • Rebuilding lost height and width:

    • Ridge augmentation and jawbone restoration rebuild areas of the jaw that have thinned over time, supporting both function and appearance.

  • Support for upper jaw implants:

    • When the upper jaw does not have enough bone height, a sinus lift adds bone below the sinus so an implant can be placed with better support.
